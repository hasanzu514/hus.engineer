# Production checklist

## Before writing

- Write one sentence explaining what the viewer will learn.
- Decide which viewer this video is for.
- Find the real story or problem that carries the explanation.
- Check whether the example must be fictional or composite.

## Before filming

- Review `channel/boundaries.md`.
- Prepare a clean filming area and synthetic demo data.
- Turn off notifications on every recorded device.
- Remove mail, paperwork, badges, photographs and client material from view.
- Confirm site or conference filming permission when needed.
- Write the opening and closing. Use bullet points for the middle.
- Make a short shot list.

## During filming

- Record the opening twice.
- Leave a pause between sections for easier editing.
- Capture room tone.
- Get each useful B-roll shot for at least 8 seconds.
- Say the thought again when the first version sounds too formal.

## Before publishing

- Watch once for the story and pacing.
- Watch again for private information in the foreground and background.
- Check names, tabs, notifications, paths, diagrams and reflections.
- Confirm that the title promises what the video delivers.
- Add sources and note fictional examples in the description.
- Ask one specific question at the end and in the pinned comment.

## After publishing

- Add the URL and publish date to the video plan.
- Record the production time.
- Save useful viewer questions in `planning/ideas.md`.
- Write what worked and what should change next time.
