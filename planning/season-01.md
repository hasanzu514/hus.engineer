# Season 1

Season 1 tests whether viewers care more about the role, the workflow tools or the combination. The sequence can change when a real site visit or conference creates a better story.

## Video 001

**Working title:** What does a process engineer in capital sales actually do?

**Purpose:** Introduce the role, mineral processing, equipment proposals and the reason for building tools.

**Status:** Outline in progress.

## Video 002

**Working title:** I built a tool because my proposals were scattered everywhere

**Purpose:** Explain the work problem behind the proposal workbench. Focus on email, documents, meetings and repeated proposal work.

**Status:** Idea.

## Video 003

**Working title:** An engineer's experience at an industry conference

**Purpose:** Show preparation, travel, the event and what happens afterward. Do not force a software lesson into the story.

**Timing:** Capture at the expected November 2026 conference. The final angle and title should come from what happens.

**Status:** Event planned.

## Video 004

**Working title:** Where I trust AI in engineering, and where I don't

**Purpose:** Explain the line between document help, deterministic calculations and engineer approval.

**Status:** Idea.

## Video 005

**Working title:** What makes an engineering RFQ difficult to work with

**Purpose:** Use a synthetic RFQ to explain missing data, conflicting values, units, installation constraints and clarification questions.

**Status:** Idea.

## Video 006

**Working title:** I kept rebuilding my engineering tools. Here is what I got wrong

**Purpose:** Discuss failed approaches, abandoned features and why a useful personal tool does not need to become a polished product.

**Fallback:** Replace this slot with a site-visit video if a suitable visit happens first. Move this idea later rather than dropping it.

**Status:** Idea.

## Questions to answer after each upload

- Did anyone ask a specific question?
- Did viewers understand what the channel covers?
- Where did people stop watching?
- Did the video attract working engineers, students or another group?
- How many hours did production take?
- What should change in the next video?
