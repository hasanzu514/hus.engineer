# Video 004: Where I trust AI in engineering, and where I don't

## Status

Idea.

## Viewer

Engineers interested in using AI without handing technical decisions to a chatbot.

## Viewer promise

The viewer will understand which parts of an engineering workflow can use AI, which parts should use fixed calculations and where an engineer must approve the result.

## Central point

AI can help interpret and organize uncertain information. It should not quietly turn uncertainty into an engineering decision.

## Example to develop

Use a synthetic RFQ with a missing value, conflicting units and an instruction hidden inside the document. Show how the workflow records uncertainty instead of inventing an answer.

## Questions to answer

- What work am I comfortable giving AI?
- What always uses deterministic calculations?
- What always requires approval?
- What mistakes have I seen AI make with engineering documents?
- How do I show the source of an extracted value?
- What happens when the information is not good enough to proceed?

## Safety notes

Use synthetic data and simplified public calculations. Do not show company prompts, rules, code or technical methods.
