# hus.engineer

This repository holds the plans, scripts, filming notes, publishing checklists and lessons for the hus.engineer channel.

The channel covers what process engineering and capital sales look like in practice. It also covers the small tools and AI workflows I use to improve repetitive engineering work.

## Folder structure

```text
channel/       Channel purpose, audience, voice and privacy rules
planning/      Season plans, ideas and viewer questions
production/    Checklists and media indexes
templates/     Reusable Markdown templates
videos/        One numbered folder per long-form video
archive/       Retired ideas and completed planning documents
```

Each video starts with one `plan.md` file. Split out a full script, research notes or a shot log only when the video becomes too large for one file.

Raw footage, audio and video exports do not belong in this repository. Store them in cloud storage or on an external drive. Put the folder link and useful clip names in the video's plan.

## Working method

1. Pick an idea from `planning/ideas.md`.
2. Copy `templates/video-plan.md` into a numbered video folder.
3. Write the viewer promise and opening before writing the rest.
4. Check every planned screen and shot against `channel/boundaries.md`.
5. Film from the plan and update it when the real story changes.
6. Record the published URL and a short postmortem in the same file.
