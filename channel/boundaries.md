# Safety and privacy boundaries

Review this file before filming a screen, office, site or conference.

## Employer and client information

- Refer to the employer as "an equipment supplier" unless a later video has clear approval to name it.
- Do not show client names, sites, engineering houses, proposal numbers or project details.
- Do not show real prices, margins, equipment cost data, proposal clauses or internal calculations.
- Do not show coworkers without their explicit permission.
- Do not imply that a personal channel speaks for the employer.

## Software demonstrations

- Record a separate demo application. Do not record the production application.
- Use a fictional company and fake users.
- Use synthetic RFQs, emails, calendars, equipment and pricing.
- Disconnect the demo from work email, production databases and internal networks.
- Hide editor sidebars, file paths, Git remotes, terminal history, notifications and browser tabs.
- Check every frame before publishing. Cropping and blurring should be a backup, not the main privacy plan.

## Sites and conferences

- Confirm that filming is allowed at each location.
- Avoid control rooms, drawings, screens, badges and conversations unless permission is clear.
- Do not record presentations or speakers without permission.
- Check the background for names, documents and equipment tags.
- Follow all PPE and site-safety rules. Filming never takes priority over the visit.

## Family and home

- It is fine to discuss being a parent and balancing family life.
- Do not show children, names, schools, routines or the home exterior.
- Do not publish travel details while the family or home is unattended.
- Check windows, mail, photographs and smart displays in home-office shots.

## Accuracy

- Do not claim that a pending proposal became an order.
- Clearly label fictional and composite examples.
- Separate personal experience from formal engineering guidance.
- Do not present simplified public examples as company methods or certified designs.
