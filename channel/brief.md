# Channel brief

## Name

hus.eng, subject to handle availability.

## What the channel is about

hus.eng shows what process engineering and capital sales look like in practice. It also explains how an engineer can use small tools and AI to improve repetitive work without building perfect commercial software.

The person and the job connect the subjects. A conference video does not need an AI section. A tool video does not need travel footage.

## Primary viewer

Working and early-career engineers in industrial roles. They understand technical work but may not write software.

This includes process engineers, technical-sales engineers and engineers who work with proposals, equipment, documents or plant data.

## Secondary viewer

Engineering students and people considering process engineering, technical sales, mining or mineral processing.

Software developers may watch the tool videos, but the channel does not write for them first.

## What viewers should say after six videos

"This person shows what the job actually looks like and how engineers can use small tools and AI to improve their workflows. The tools do not need to become huge software products."

## Content balance

The first six videos will test two subjects:

- The role, site visits, conferences and working life
- Engineering workflows, small tools, AI and lessons from building them

Family life appears as context. It is not a separate family-vlog subject.

## Tone

Calm, conversational, reflective, technical and direct. Explain enough for a non-programmer while giving engineers something concrete to learn.

## Publishing pace

One long-form YouTube video each month. Aim for 6 to 10 minutes at first. Make a Short or Reel only when part of the long video works on its own.

## What success means

The first six videos succeed if a few people ask real questions, describe their own workflow problems or return for another video. Useful messages matter more than broad but shallow views.
