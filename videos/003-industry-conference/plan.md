# Video 003: An engineer's experience at an industry conference

## Status

Event planned for November 2026.

## Viewer

Students and engineers who have not attended an industry conference or want to see what one is useful for.

## Viewer promise

Decide after the event. The video should answer a real question raised by the trip instead of becoming a generic travel montage.

## Possible angles

- What an engineer does before, during and after a conference
- What I expected compared with what was useful
- How I decide who to speak with and which sessions to attend
- What happens to conference notes after I get home
- Balancing travel, normal work and family responsibilities

## Capture list

- Planning and packing at home
- Travel footage without live location details
- Venue exterior and permitted public areas
- Badge with private details covered
- Short reflections away from crowded rooms
- Notes at the end of each day
- Follow-up work after returning home

## Questions to answer before travel

- What conference is it and what can be shown?
- Why am I attending?
- What work continues while I am away?
- What do I hope to learn or accomplish?
- Which filming rules apply?

## Safety notes

Do not record private conversations, presentation material, badges, client information or people who have not agreed to appear.
