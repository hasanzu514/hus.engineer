# Repository instructions

This is a writing and production-planning repository for hus.engineer. Keep the files useful to a person preparing a video. Do not turn the repo into a software project.

## Writing voice

- Use plain, human language.
- Sound calm, conversational, reflective, technical and direct.
- Prefer short sentences when an idea is simple.
- Use first person when writing in Hasan's voice.
- Avoid em dashes, decorative punctuation, puffery and promotional language.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Do not force every point into a list of three.
- Cut chatbot phrases, filler, vague claims and generic conclusions.
- Name the real action, decision or fact. Avoid abstract jargon.
- Do not make a script sound more polished than the speaker.
- Read spoken lines aloud in your head. Rewrite anything Hasan would not naturally say.

## Truth and privacy

- Never invent a real project outcome. Label fictional or composite examples.
- Do not include client names, proposal numbers, prices, contacts or process data.
- Do not copy confidential company documents, code, calculations or screens into this repository.
- Use a fictional company, synthetic RFQs and invented data in public demos.
- Do not show children, coworkers or private family details.
- Treat site and conference filming permission as separate from permission to discuss the job.

## File conventions

- Number long-form video folders with three digits.
- Keep one `plan.md` per video until a separate file has a clear use.
- Update `planning/season-01.md` and the table in `README.md` when video status changes.
- Put raw footage and exports outside Git. Store links and clip notes in the video plan.
- Preserve useful rejected ideas in `planning/ideas.md` or `archive/`.
