# Video 005: What makes an engineering RFQ difficult to work with

## Status

Idea.

## Viewer

Students, early-career engineers and people who send or review technical equipment requests.

## Viewer promise

The viewer will see why an RFQ can look complete while still lacking the information needed for a safe technical proposal.

## Central point

The hard part is often separating what the documents say, what we assume and what we still need to ask.

## Synthetic example

Create a fictional sampler RFQ for an existing plant. Include process data, an old layout drawing and a request for particle-size sampling. Leave out reliable installation dimensions and include one conflicting flow value.

## Questions to answer

- Which documents normally arrive?
- What is often missing?
- Which contradictions matter most?
- How do old drawings differ from the plant as built?
- What questions require a site visit?
- What makes a sample representative?
- How do I record assumptions before preparing a proposal?

## Safety notes

Label the RFQ as fictional. Do not reuse a client document as the template.
